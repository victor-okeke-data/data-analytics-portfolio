# 📊 Project 1: Business Sales Analysis - LPG Distribution

## 🏢 Business Context
As a Data Analyst for a regional Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) distribution company in Rivers State, I was tasked by the Operations Manager to analyze a recent extract of sales data. The objective was to identify top-performing locations, understand customer segmentation revenue, and determine the highest-moving cylinder sizes to optimize inventory.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Applied
* **Microsoft Excel:** Primary tool used for end-to-end data processing.
* **Data Cleaning:** Utilized `TRIM()` and `PROPER()` functions to standardize messy text entries. Standardized inconsistent date formats into a uniform `DD-MMM-YYYY` structure using Text-to-Columns.
* **Data Transformation:** Calculated missing financial metrics using basic arithmetic formulas and applied standard accounting formats.
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Built PivotTables and PivotCharts to rapidly aggregate and visualize key performance indicators.

## 📈 Key Business Insights
1. **Top Revenue Location:** Obio-Akpor generated the highest revenue at ₦499,500, outperforming Port Harcourt and Eleme.
2. **Customer Segmentation:** Commercial clients overwhelmingly drove business revenue compared to Residential clients, accounting for the vast majority of sales value.
3. **Inventory Movement:** The 50kg cylinder was the highest-moving product by volume, with 23 total units sold. 

## 💡 Recommendations
* **Focus on Commercial Contracts:** Since commercial clients are the primary revenue drivers, the sales team should prioritize B2B (Business-to-Business) acquisition strategies in Obio-Akpor and Port Harcourt.
* **Inventory Prioritization:** Logistics and warehousing must prioritize the stocking and transportation of 50kg cylinders to prevent stockouts of the highest-demand product.
