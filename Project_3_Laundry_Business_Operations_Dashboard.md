# Project 3: Laundry Business Operations Dashboard

## 📌 Objective
To build an interactive financial dashboard that allows business owners to instantly analyze revenue by service and customer type.

## 🛠️ Skills & Tools Used
*   **Data Aggregation (PivotTables):** Summarized raw daily transaction logs into high-level revenue metrics without manual calculation.
*   **Data Visualization (PivotCharts):** Created dynamic column charts to visually compare the profitability of different laundry services.
*   **Interactive Filtering (Slicers):** Implemented one-click filtering buttons, enabling the separation of Walk-in and Corporate revenue streams in real-time.
